Good morning! Don't worry, it's a common confusion. Let's make it simple:

Affect (verb/செயல்)

To influence or change something.
Example: The rain will affect our game.

Effect (noun/பெயர்ச்சொல்)

The result of a change.
Example: The effect of the rain was a muddy field.
