Reading comprehension   Exam Passage: The Life of Honeybees Honeybees are highly social insects that live in well-organized colonies called beehives. A single hive can contain up to 60,000 bees, divided into three distinct types: the Queen, the Workers, and the Drones. The Queen is the largest bee and is responsible for laying thousands of eggs daily. The female Worker bees do all the actual work, such as collecting pollen from flowers, building the honeycomb, and protecting the hive from enemies. The male Drones exist solely to mate with the queen. Bees communicate with each other using a special series of movements known as the "waggle dance," which tells other hive members the exact location of food sources. Question 3:  Mention any two duties performed by the female Worker bees. Answer:  Two duties performed by the Worker bees are collecting pollen from flowers and protecting the hive from enemies. Question 4:  How do honeybees communicate the location of food sources to one another? Answer:  Honeybees communicate the location of food sources using a special series of movements known as the "waggle dance."
